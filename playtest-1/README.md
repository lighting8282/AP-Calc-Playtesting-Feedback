# Playtest 1

**Started:** November 2026
**Build:** 0.4.0-pt (browser), on an invite-only itch.io page

## What's being tested

The full story and the card shop:
- High school: Calcy's intro, the first duels, and the rival at the gate
- The college campus and all five wings, choosing a major, and every degree up to the Doctorate
- The card shop (packs, rarities and the Extra Credit economy)
- Practice mode

Arcade, Golf and the Gauntlet are switched off for this round so testers stay on the story.

## What we most want to learn

1. Does the intro make the first duel clear?
2. Which fights feel unfair or confusing, and why?
3. Does the shop feel worth the Extra Credit, or does progress stall waiting for it?
4. Where do people stop playing?
